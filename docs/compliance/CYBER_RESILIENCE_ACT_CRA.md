# European Union Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) Compliance Guide

## 1. Scope
The EU **Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)** imposes mandatory cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements across their lifecycle.

---

## 2. Essential Requirements Mapping
- **Annex I Part 1 (Security by Design)**: Distroless runtime, non-root execution, defense-in-depth security headers.
- **Annex I Part 2 (Vulnerability Handling)**: Documented Vulnerability Disclosure Policy (VDP) with defined response SLAs.
- **Annex II (Technical Documentation & SBOM)**: Machine-readable CycloneDX 1.5 SBOM generated with every build.
